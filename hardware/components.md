# Hardware

## Main Controller

- Arduino Uno

## Soil Monitoring

- 2 × Soil moisture sensors

The two sensors are installed at different locations in the irrigation area to obtain more representative soil moisture measurements.

## Water Management

### Clean Water Reservoir

- 1 × Water reservoir
- 1 × Water level sensor
- 1 × Water pump

### Recycled Water Reservoir

- 1 × Water reservoir
- 1 × Water level sensor
- 1 × Water pump

The recycled water reservoir collects water after the irrigation process.

## User Interface

- 1 × 16x2 I2C LCD

The LCD displays information such as soil moisture values, reservoir status and system state.

## Presence Detection

- 1 × Ultrasonic sensor

The sensor detects the presence of a person in front of the test bench.

## Lighting

- 12V LED lighting
- Relay module

The lighting is activated when a person is detected in front of the bench.

## Control

- Relay modules for pump and lighting control

## Test Bench

The physical test bench is made from CNC-cut PVC panels.

The modular design allows students to easily connect and disconnect the different components during practical sessions.
